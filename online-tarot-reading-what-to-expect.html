What to Expect From an Online Tarot Reading

A gentle guide to what happens, how long to book, and what it costs.

← Back to Blog
By Jess Hollamby November 2026 5 min read

If you've never had a tarot reading, it's normal to wonder what you're walking into. Will it feel strange? Will you have to talk a lot? Will someone tell you something you're not ready to hear?

Here's what actually happens in an online reading with me, so you can decide whether it feels right for you.

The short answer: a tarot reading is a 15, 30 or 45 minute online session where I draw cards and share what they bring up about what's active in your life right now. It costs $40, $60 or $75. Book a tarot reading or keep reading to see what to expect.

What is a tarot reading?

Tarot is a deck of 78 cards, each with its own imagery and meaning. In a reading, the cards give your situation a shape you can look at. I tune into what's present for you and use the cards to bring it into focus.

Tarot tends to work with current energy - what's active right now, what's around a situation, what possibilities are in motion. That makes it a good fit when you want clarity on one question or one area of your life.

What happens in an online reading?

We meet online at your booked time, so you can be wherever feels comfortable. No preparation needed - just show up.

We start with a short check-in about what's on your mind. You can bring a specific question, an area of your life, or nothing in particular. Then I draw the cards and talk you through what I see.

Each session follows what comes up, not a script. You're fully present throughout, so you can ask questions, tell me what lands and what doesn't, and take it wherever it needs to go. It's a conversation.

Which length should I choose?

  • 15 minutes - 3 card reading, $40. A focused reading for one question or one area of your life.
  • 30 minutes, $60. An in-depth reading with more room to follow what comes up.
  • 45 minutes, $75. An extended reading for deeper exploration.

If you're new to tarot and not sure, the 3 card reading is a gentle, low-commitment way to try it.

What a reading can offer

A tarot reading gives you perspective. It can help you name something you were already sensing, or look at a decision from a new angle.

It doesn't replace medical, legal or financial advice, and the decisions always stay with you.

Who is it for?

  • You're curious about energy work and want a gentle place to start
  • You have a decision or a question you keep circling
  • You feel stuck and want a fresh view of what's going on
  • You've been giving everything to everyone and want a little time that's about you

You don't need any spiritual background or any belief in tarot. Curiosity is enough.

Common questions about online tarot readings

How much does an online tarot reading cost?

A 15 minute 3 card reading is $40, a 30 minute reading is $60 and a 45 minute reading is $75. You can pay via Stripe (Zip, Klarna and Afterpay are available) or PayPal (Pay in 4 is available).

Do I need to believe in tarot?

No. Curiosity is enough, and you don't need any spiritual background.

Will you tell me my future?

Tarot works with the energy around a situation and the possibilities in motion. It shows what's active right now, and the choices stay with you.

Can I book from outside Adelaide?

Yes. Readings are held online and are available worldwide.

How is tarot different from a psychic or Akashic reading?

Tarot works with current energy and suits a specific question or decision. A psychic reading draws on direct intuitive perception, and an Akashic Record reading goes deeper into the soul-level patterns underneath. You can read more about psychic readings and Akashic Record readings.
