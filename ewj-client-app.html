Empath Field Guide
What is energy absorption? ▾
Energy absorption happens when you unconsciously take on the emotional, energetic, or physical state of someone else. As an empath, your nervous system is highly attuned to the people around you - this is a gift, but without awareness it can leave you carrying feelings and sensations that aren't yours. The first step is noticing it's happening.
Signs your nervous system is overwhelmed ▾
Your nervous system signals overwhelm through the body: tight chest, shallow breathing, a racing or heavy feeling, sudden fatigue, irritability out of proportion to the situation, difficulty making decisions, or a sense of wanting to disappear. These aren't weaknesses. They're your system asking for space.
Empathy vs absorption - the difference ▾
Empathy is feeling WITH someone - you understand their pain but remain in your own body. Absorption is feeling AS someone - their state becomes indistinguishable from yours. Empaths often default to absorption without realising it. The distinction matters because empathy is sustainable; absorption is exhausting and leads to burnout.
Closing your energy field (quick guide) ▾
Take three slow breaths. With each exhale, imagine your energy drawing closer to your body - like pulling a cloak inward. Set a quiet intention: "My energy is mine. I'm here, in my body, in my own field." You don't need to shut down or block anything. Just return to yourself. This takes less than 60 seconds and can be done anywhere.
Why empaths get tired around people ▾
Empaths process environmental energy constantly, even passively. Being around people - especially in groups or emotionally charged situations - is work for the empath nervous system. This is not social anxiety or introversion (though it can coexist). It's energetic processing. The tiredness is real. Rest is productive for empaths in a way it isn't for everyone else.
Protection Practice
The Field Reset
A 2-minute practice for before, during, or after any interaction.
1
Feel your feet on the ground. Take one slow breath in and out.
2
Notice where in your body you feel any tension or weight. Don't try to change it yet - just notice.
3
Breathe in and imagine drawing your own energy back to you from wherever it's scattered. Breathe out anything that isn't yours.
4
Set your field. Imagine a soft boundary around your body - not a wall, just a membrane. It lets love in and keeps everything else out.
5
Say quietly: "I am here. This is mine. That is theirs."
