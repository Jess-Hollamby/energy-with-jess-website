Business Policy Templates

Three ready-to-customise policy templates for your solo business. Replace every [placeholder] with your own details.

How to use these templates: These cover the three core policies your business needs. They are starting points - not legal advice. Once you've customised them, consider having a lawyer or business advisor review them before publishing, especially if you provide health-related services.

Add them to your website, include them in your client intake pack, or share them when requested. The more clearly your policies are communicated upfront, the fewer awkward conversations later.

Privacy Policy

[Business Name] respects your right to privacy and is committed to safeguarding the personal information of our clients and website visitors. We adhere to the Australian Privacy Principles contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy sets out how we collect and handle your personal information.

"Personal information" means any information we hold that is identifiable as being about you.

What information we collect

We may collect personal information including, but not limited to:

  • Your name, phone number, address and email address
  • Information you provide when making a booking or enquiry
  • Health or wellbeing information relevant to the services you receive
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)
  • Any other information you choose to share with us

How we collect your information

We collect personal information when you:

  • Make a booking, purchase, or enquiry
  • Complete an intake or consent form
  • Interact with us in person, by phone, or by email
  • Visit our website or sign up to our email list

How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide and manage the services you've booked
  • Send booking confirmations, receipts, and relevant updates
  • Improve our services based on your needs
  • Contact you by email, phone, or SMS where relevant
  • Send you newsletters or promotional content where you have opted in

Disclosure of your information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information only where:

  • It is required for the delivery of our services (e.g. payment processors)
  • Required by law, court order, or government authority
  • You have given us permission to do so

Security

[Business Name] takes reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as completely secure. You transmit information to us at your own risk.

Accessing and updating your information

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date. To make a request, contact us at [your email address].

Website and cookies

When you visit our website ([your website URL]), we may collect general usage information such as browser type and pages visited. We may use cookies to improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some site functionality.

Complaints about privacy

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us at [your email address]. We take privacy complaints seriously and will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted to our website and take effect immediately upon posting. Last updated: [date].

Refund & Cancellation Policy

By booking a service with [Business Name], you agree to the following cancellation and refund terms. Please read this before making a booking.

Note: The timeframes and percentages below are examples - adjust them to match what works for your business before publishing. Be consistent, and make sure clients see your policy before they book.

Cancellations

  • More than [48 hours] notice: Full refund, or rescheduled at no charge.
  • Between [24 - 48 hours] notice: A cancellation fee of [50%] of the booking total will apply.
  • Less than [24 hours] notice: The full booking amount is non-refundable.

Where a cancellation fee applies and you have already paid, any remaining balance after the fee is deducted will be held as a credit and applied to a future booking.

No-shows

If you do not attend your appointment without notice, the full session fee is non-refundable. Repeated no-shows ([e.g. 3 or more]) may result in prepayment being required for future bookings.

Early termination of a session

If you choose to end a session early after it has commenced, the full fee is payable.

Change of mind

We do not offer refunds for change of mind once a service has been delivered or a digital product has been accessed.

Digital products and courses

Due to the nature of digital products and online courses, all sales are final once access has been granted. If you experience a technical issue preventing access, please contact us at [your email address] and we will work to resolve it.

If you offer a guarantee: Include it here - e.g. "We offer a [7-day] satisfaction guarantee on [specific product/service]. If you are not satisfied, contact us within [7 days] of purchase." Only include a guarantee if you genuinely offer one.

Gift cards and vouchers

Gift cards and vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash. They are valid for [12 months] from the date of purchase.

Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Where a service has a major failure, you are entitled to a refund or remedy as provided by law.

How to request a refund or cancellation

All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted in writing to [your email address]. Please include your name, booking date, and the reason for your request.
