Solid Ground - Module 3

Brand Breakdown

Your brand in one place. Fill this in and come back to it whenever you create something new.

Brand isn't a logo. It's the feeling someone has when they find you for the first time - the colours they see, the words they read, the sense of whether this is for them or not. Getting clear on your basics early means everything you create points in the same direction. Fill in what you know. Leave blank what you don't yet - that's fine.
01

Your Business

Doesn't have to be clever. "Reiki for women who need to breathe again" is enough.
02

Your Colours

You don't need more than 3-4 colours. One primary, one accent, one dark, one light background is plenty. Pick colours that feel like you - not what's trendy.

03

Your Fonts

Two fonts is enough - one for headings, one for body text. More than two starts to look messy. Google Fonts has thousands of free options. Canva also has a solid library built in.
Usually a serif - elegant and warm
Usually a sans-serif - clean and readable
04

Your Voice

Voice is how you sound in writing. It shapes everything - captions, emails, your website, how you describe what you do. Getting clear on your voice means your content stops sounding like everyone else's.
e.g. warm, grounded, direct - how do you want people to feel when they read your words?
Language that feels like you. Words you reach for naturally.
Jargon, hype language, words that make you cringe. If it doesn't sound like you, add it here.
