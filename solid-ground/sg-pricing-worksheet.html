Solid Ground - Module 11

Pricing Worksheet

Work out what you need, what the market charges, and what to put on your services page.

Pricing is one of the most emotionally loaded parts of starting out. Most practitioners underprice themselves - not because their work isn't worth more, but because they haven't done this part clearly. Go through each section. The numbers give you something to stand on.
Part 1 What You Actually Need
Before you can price anything, you need to know your floor - the minimum you need to bring in each month to cover your life. This isn't your goal; it's your safety net number.

Say your price out loud before anyone asks you. In the mirror, in the car, to a friend. The wobble in your voice when you say your own rate is the wobble your client feels. Practice until it comes out steady.
Part 4 Sense Check
A few final questions to make sure your pricing works before you publish it anywhere.
Does your rate cover your monthly expenses? (check Part 1)
Is your rate above the floor number you calculated?
Does your rate sit somewhere in the market range you found?
Would you feel comfortable if a potential client asked you your price right now?
Your pricing today is not your pricing forever. You get to raise it. Set a date to revisit it.
