Your First 30 Days

A week-by-week checklist for what to actually do when you're starting. Not everything at once — just what matters, in the right order.

0 of 20 complete
Week 1
The Foundation
  • Apply for your ABN at abr.gov.au — it's free and takes 10 minutes
  • Check your business name is available at asic.gov.au
  • Register your business name if needed ($39/year) — or trade under your own name for now (that's fine)
  • Open a separate bank account for business income and expenses
  • Set up a free Google account — Calendar, Drive, Docs will run your business
Week 2
Get Paid + Get Visible
  • Set up Stripe or PayPal — free until you make money, then they take a small % per transaction
  • Download the Invoice Template from this course and add your details
  • Set up your Instagram business profile — photo, bio, link
  • Write your bio: what you do, who it's for, how to get in touch
  • Create a Canva account (free tier is enough)
Week 3
First Clients
  • Tell 5 people in your life what you're doing — friends, family, former colleagues
  • Post something on social media. It doesn't have to be perfect. Done beats polished.
  • Send one personal message to someone who might need your service
  • Say your price out loud. Three times. Notice where the resistance sits.
  • Ask one person for a testimonial or referral if you've worked together before
Week 4
Review + Reset
  • Open your Expense Tracker and log everything from the month
  • Look back at Week 1-3: what worked? What do you want to repeat?
  • Identify your next 3 potential clients
  • Set a simple plan for month 2 — just 3 priorities, not a full strategy
  • Celebrate what you've actually done. You started. That's not nothing.
You don't need everything sorted before you start. You need a way to be found, a way to get paid, and one person who knows you exist. The rest builds from there.
