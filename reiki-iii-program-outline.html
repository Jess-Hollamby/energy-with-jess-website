Reiki Master

Sessions 1 - 4
Reiki Master Session 1 of 7

Foundation + Shielding

We start here together - grounding in, settling in, and building a clear foundation before we go deeper. This session revisits everything you learned in Reiki I and II through a Master-level lens, so you can see how the layers connect. Then we move into shielding and protection: cord cutting, the bubble and egg shield, grounding practices, and psychic protection. Each technique is walked through with guided practice so you leave with tools you can actually use, not just concepts you've read about.

Reiki Master Session 2 of 7

Symbols + Attunement

This is the session at the heart of your Master training. We begin with the Usui lineage - where this practice comes from and how it has moved through practitioners to reach you. You'll be introduced to two new symbols: Dai Ko Myo and Raku. We take time with both - how to draw them, what they carry, and how they work in practice. There is an integration meditation before we move into your Master attunement. Afterwards, we talk through what the 21-day integration period looks and feels like, and what to expect as the energy settles.

A note on the 21-day integration

After your Master attunement in Session 2, you'll move through a 21-day integration period. This is normal and expected - energy shifts can feel different for everyone. Rest, drink water, notice what comes up. You don't need to do anything specific. Jess is available between sessions if questions arise.

Reiki Master Session 3 of 7

TCM + 5 Elements

This session brings in Traditional Chinese Medicine and the 5 Elements - Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water - in a way that's accessible and immediately usable, not academic. You'll learn how each element maps to body systems, emotions, seasons, and organs, and how this framework can help you read what a client might need within a session. This adds a practical layer to how you listen and respond when you're working with someone on the table.

Reiki Master Session 4 of 7

Integration + Closing

Your final Master session begins with an integration check-in - how have the weeks felt, what has landed, what still has questions. From there we move into the responsibilities that come with Master-level training: ethics in practice and how those deepen at this level. For those intending to see clients professionally, we cover the business basics you'll need to get started. The session closes with a full practice run, and your Reiki Master certificate is presented.

Reiki Master / Teacher

Sessions 5 - 8

These four sessions follow directly on from the four Reiki Master sessions above. You don't need to make a decision about the Teacher pathway before the program begins - it can be added once you're in.

Master / Teacher Session 5 of 8

Teaching Reiki I

The Teacher pathway begins here with something worth sitting with: what it actually means to pass this on. This session is built around learning, observing, and practising the Reiki I attunement protocols, alongside the practical side of structuring a Reiki I class. We also look at what it takes to teach online - your space, your Zoom setup, and how to hold the container for students through a screen. You'll begin building your own teaching manual in this session, which becomes your ongoing reference as you grow.

Master / Teacher Session 6 of 8

Teaching Reiki II

Session 6 moves into Reiki II attunement protocols - learn, observe, practise - alongside how to structure and run a Reiki II class. A key part of this session is distant attunements: the empty chair method using HSZSN, and how to offer this effectively for students who aren't in the room with you. We also look at what to watch for when running Reiki II online, and where students often need extra support. Your teaching manual continues to build.

Master / Teacher Session 7 of 8

Teaching Reiki III + Graduation

The final session covers Reiki III attunement protocols - learn, observe, and practise - and how to structure your own Reiki III class. We use the STICK method for curriculum design, so you leave with a clear, practical approach to building course content that holds. From there: the business foundations for teachers - pricing, intake processes, client care, and holding your boundaries as someone passing on a lineage. We close by completing your teaching manual, and the session ends with certificate presentation and graduation.
