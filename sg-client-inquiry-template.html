Client Inquiry Templates

What to say when someone reaches out — so you don't lose the booking because you didn't know how to reply.

The rule: Reply within 24 hours. That's it. People who enquire are in a moment of readiness — the longer you wait, the more that window closes. You don't need a perfect response. You need a prompt one.
Template 1
First Reply to an Inquiry
Email / DM Response
Subject: Re: [their name] — [your service]
Hi [Name], Thanks so much for reaching out — I'd love to tell you a bit more. [One sentence about your service and who it's for — e.g. "I work with women who are exhausted from giving everything to everyone, helping them find space to breathe again through Reiki and intuitive healing."] Here's what a session looks like: - [Duration and format — e.g. "60 minutes, online via Zoom"] - [What happens — e.g. "We start with a brief check-in, then I hold space for whatever needs to move"] - [What they walk away with — e.g. "A recording of the session and a short voice note with what I noticed"] Sessions are $[price]. [Add availability — e.g. "I have space on Tuesdays and Thursdays at the moment."] The easiest way to book is [booking link or payment link]. Any questions, just reply here — I'm happy to chat before you commit to anything. Warm, [Your name]
Template 2
Follow-Up (3-7 Days Later)
Most people don't follow up because it feels pushy. It's not. They're busy. A gentle nudge shows you care enough to check in — and often that's all it takes.
Follow-Up Email / DM
Subject: Just checking in — [your service]
Hi [Name], Just circling back in case my last message got buried! [One line reminder — e.g. "I still have space in my schedule if you're still thinking about it."] No pressure either way — happy to answer any questions if that helps. [Your name]
Template 3
When They Say "I'll Think About It"
Response to "I'll Think About It"
Subject: Re: [your service]
Of course — take your time. I'm here when you're ready. If any questions come up in the meantime, just reply. [Optional: add something relevant — e.g. "I do have limited spots in July, so let me know if timing matters for you."] [Your name]
Practitioner Add-Ons
Swap these into Template 1 where it says "Here's what a session looks like" — or add them as extra context below your main reply.
Energy Healing / Intuitive Healing
I work intuitively — meaning each session is shaped around what comes up for you on the day, rather than a set structure. Sessions are held [in person / online]. For distance sessions, you don't need to do anything specific — just find a quiet space where you can rest for the duration. After the session I'll send you a voice note with what I noticed and anything that came through.
Reiki
Reiki is a gentle energy healing practice that works with your body's natural ability to find balance. You don't need to believe in it for it to work — you just need to be open to it. [In person:] You'll lie fully clothed on a massage table. I'll place my hands lightly on or above your body. Most people feel deeply relaxed, some notice warmth or tingling, some drift off. [Distance:] You'll be in your own space. I'll hold the session energetically — it's just as effective. Most people find it easier to relax at home.
Tarot / Psychic Readings
Readings are [15 / 30 / 45 / 60] minutes. We connect [via Zoom / via voice note / in person]. You don't need to prepare questions — but if there's something specific you want to explore, feel free to mention it at the start. [Optional:] I'll record the session so you can come back to it. The link is available for 30 days.
Massage / Myotherapy
Sessions are held at [your location / clinic name / address]. What to bring: comfortable clothing to change into, or you're welcome to undress to your comfort level — you'll be draped with a sheet throughout. Please arrive 5 minutes early for your first session so we can do a brief intake. [If relevant:] If you have any injuries, chronic conditions, or areas to avoid, just let me know before we start.
