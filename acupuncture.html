What This Is

A system built on the whole of you

Acupuncture is part of Traditional Chinese Medicine - a system of medicine practiced for thousands of years that looks at the body as a whole rather than a collection of separate parts. Using fine needles placed at specific points, it works with the body's natural processes to support balance and ease.

Each session starts with a full intake and assessment - looking at what's happening in the body, what's contributing to it, and how to best support it. That shapes the treatment. No two sessions are the same, because no two people are.

Your body already knows how to find balance. Sometimes it just needs a little help remembering.

Jess brings her background in energy work and her deep understanding of the nervous system into her acupuncture practice. It's clinical and grounded, and it also holds the full picture of who you are - not just the symptom in front of her.
