Myotherapy

Hands-on work for a body that's been carrying more than it should.

What This Is

Your body holds everything

Myotherapy is a form of physical therapy focused on assessing and addressing musculoskeletal pain and dysfunction. Using a range of hands-on techniques, it works with what your body is holding - tension, restriction, and patterns that have built up over time.

Each session starts with a full assessment - looking at what's happening, what's contributing to it, and what your body actually needs. Treatment is shaped from there. No scripts, no one-size-fits-all approach.

Your body isn't the problem. It's been adapting. This work gives it space to let go.

Beyond the structural, this work also restores energy flow. When we work through the tissues, bones and sinews, we're not just addressing physical restriction - we're clearing the pathways that energy moves through. That's where the deeper shifts happen.

Whether you're dealing with ongoing pain, reduced range of movement, or a body that just feels disconnected and tight - this is grounded, physical work. Sometimes that's exactly what's needed.

Sessions are in person in Adelaide. If you're not sure whether myotherapy is right for what you're dealing with, reach out and we can talk through it.

What's Included

Techniques used in session

🤲

Remedial Massage

Deep, targeted work on muscles, fascia, and connective tissue. Addresses tension patterns and helps restore normal tissue function.

🎯

Trigger Point Therapy

Locating and releasing tight bands of muscle that cause localised and referred pain. Precise, effective work for stubborn tension.

🪡

Dry Needling

Fine needles applied directly to trigger points to reduce pain, release tension, and improve range of movement at a deeper level.

🫧

Cupping

Suction cups applied to the skin to lift the tissue, improve circulation, and release muscle tightness - particularly effective for the back, shoulders, and hips.

🔄

Mobilisation

Gentle, guided joint and soft tissue movement to improve range of motion, reduce stiffness, and restore ease in everyday movement.

Who It's For

Range of movement

Stiffness, restriction, and that feeling of your body not moving the way it used to. Myotherapy works with the tissue to restore ease.

Women's health

Pelvic health, hormonal patterns, postural changes through different life stages. Physical support for what women carry in the body.

Head, neck and jaw

Headaches, neck tension, jaw holding. Work that addresses the full picture rather than just the site of pain.

Breathing and chest
